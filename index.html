Hi, I'm Shalom, a design engineer 👋

What does that mean?

Design engineering sits between product design and frontend engineering, combining the thinking behind an experience with the technical work of building it.

For me, curiosity and creativity have always been closely connected. They show up in different ways depending on what I'm working on, but I keep returning to the same process: questioning what exists, exploring what could be different, and turning those ideas into something tangible.

Design engineering gives that process a place to take shape. I can think, question, build, and refine without treating design and code as separate disciplines.

The products I create reflect that. They are intentional in how they feel and rigorous in how they function.
