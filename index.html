Hi, I’m Shalom, a design engineer 👋

What does that mean?

It makes more sense with a little context. I started in creative work. Writing, visual art, choir. Then I moved into code, and it felt like the same discipline expressed through a different medium. The goal never changed. It was always about shaping ideas into something others can experience.

That perspective stayed with me. I do not separate design from engineering. I approach both as one continuous process of thinking, building, and refining.

The products I create reflect that. They are intentional in how they feel and rigorous in how they function.

By SHALOM DONGA Special to The Maker's Times
